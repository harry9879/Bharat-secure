# Red-flag guide (given to the LLM on every check)

## Scam types
- **phishing_otp**: the caller wants a secret or control of the phone: OTP, UPI/ATM PIN, password, CVV, card number, "verification code", Aadhaar/PAN details through a link, installing AnyDesk/TeamViewer/"support" apps, clicking an SMS/WhatsApp link to "update KYC" or "unblock" an account.
- **digital_arrest**: the caller claims to be police, CBI, ED, NCB, customs, cyber crime, TRAI, a court or the tax department, accuses you (drugs parcel, money laundering, Aadhaar/SIM misuse, illegal transaction, legal notice), threatens arrest, FIR, warrant or legal action, keeps you isolated (stay on video call, don't disconnect, don't tell family), and asks for money as "verification", "security deposit", "settlement" or transfer to an "RBI safe account".
- **banking**: the trick runs through payments: entering a UPI PIN or scanning a QR to "receive" money, a "refund" via request-money, "I sent you money by mistake, send it back", a fake bank or loan app asking for a processing fee before a loan, cheque/overpayment tricks.
- **other_scam**: everything else that asks for money under pressure: lottery/KBC prize fees, job/registration or task-based job fees, fake relative or friend in an emergency, kidnapping/accident stories, sextortion or threats to leak photos, electricity/gas disconnection "tonight", guaranteed-return investments, insurance bonus fees, passport/subsidy "charges".

## Strong red flags (any of these is very suspicious)
1. Asks for an OTP, PIN, CVV, password or a code sent by SMS **to be told to the caller**.
2. Police / CBI / court / customs on a phone or video call threatening arrest. **Real police never arrest anyone over a call and never ask for money.**
3. "Don't disconnect", "stay on video", "don't tell anyone / family".
4. Transfer money to a "safe", "secure", "RBI" or "verification" account.
5. Enter a UPI PIN or scan a QR to **receive** money. (A PIN is only ever needed to **send** money.)
6. A fee must be paid first to receive a prize, loan, job, refund, bonus or subsidy.
7. Extreme urgency: "10 minutes", "today itself", "tonight your connection will be cut".
8. Click a link / install an app to "verify", "update KYC" or "unblock".

## Genuine calls (do NOT flag these)
- Banks calling to **warn** you ("we will never ask for your OTP or PIN"), confirm a transaction you made, remind about an EMI or due date, or tell you a card is dispatched, with no request for secrets or money to a new account.
- Police calling about a complaint **you** filed, a witness statement, passport or tenant verification, asking you to visit the station, with no payment over the phone.
- Delivery agents, cab drivers, hospitals, schools, hotels, airlines, HR/interview scheduling, college placement, friends and family making plans.
- Informational / awareness calls that point you to an **official** website or office.
A genuine call can mention bank, police, OTP, account, parcel or payment; that alone is **not** a red flag. What matters is whether the caller asks **you** for a secret, money, or an app/link action under pressure.
